The Story of Nashik

Nashik’s spiritual resonance lies deeply intertwined with the eternal flow of the Godavari. As the revered source of this sacred river, the land is blessed by the divine presence of the Trimbakeshwar temple.

It is here, amidst the timeless sanctity of Panchavati where the Ramayana unfolded, that our journey begins. Every drop captured reflects a profound heritage, bringing the tranquil essence of Nashik’s sacred grounds into your home.

FAQ'S

Is this authentic Ganga Jal? expand_more

Yes, our product contains authentic Ganga Jal sourced with deep reverence and adherence to traditional practices. We ensure full transparency from source to your sacred space.

Where is the water sourced from? expand_more

The sacred water is sourced from the Godavari River in Nashik, a location of profound spiritual significance where the river originates.

How is it collected? expand_more

Our collection process follows ancient guidelines, honoring the purity of the source. Water is collected with utmost care to maintain its sacred integrity.

How is it processed/filled? expand_more

The water undergoes stringent quality checks to ensure its purity and safety, all while preserving its sacred essence before being meticulously filled into our premium vessels.

Is it lab tested? expand_more

Yes, every batch is rigorously tested in certified laboratories to guarantee safety and authenticity.

Can I see the laboratory report? expand_more

Yes, we provide access to relevant laboratory reports upon request or via product verification codes.

How can I verify my product? expand_more

Each vessel includes a unique serial ID and a verification tag. You can verify the authenticity and batch details through our digital platform.

Why is the product priced at ₹999? expand_more

The price reflects the exquisite craftsmanship of the collectible vessel, the authentic sourcing and testing process, and the premium presentation designed to honor this sacred element.

Why is the Sacred Duo ₹1,800? expand_more

The Sacred Duo offers exceptional value for two vessels, recognizing that many choose to keep one for their home and gift the other to a loved one.

What makes the bottle collectible? expand_more

Our bottles are designed with premium materials like 24K gold plating and hand-molded ceramic, featuring detailed architectural motifs inspired by sacred temples, making them beautiful additions to any spiritual space.

What is the Limited Collection? expand_more

The Limited Collection features highly exclusive, numbered editions presented in luxurious teak wood and velvet packaging, intended for discerning collectors and momentous occasions.

How should I store the product? expand_more

We recommend storing the vessel in a clean, sacred space such as a home temple or altar, away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures, to preserve its integrity and presentation.

Do you ship across India? expand_more

Yes, we offer secure shipping to locations across India, ensuring your sacred product arrives safely.

What happens if the product arrives damaged? expand_more

In the rare event of transit damage, please contact our support team immediately. We will arrange a prompt replacement to ensure you receive a perfect product.

What is your replacement policy? expand_more

We replace any items that are defective or damaged upon arrival. Please refer to our detailed replacement policy in the Legal section for instructions.

Do you accept returns? expand_more

Due to the sacred nature of the product, we typically do not accept returns. We offer replacements for damaged or defective items as outlined in our policy.
